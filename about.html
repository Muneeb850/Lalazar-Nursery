Our story

A nursery for people who love green.

Lalazar Nursery began with a simple idea: everyone deserves a little green in their life. We raise plants in our own greenhouse, acclimatise them to real homes, and share everything we’ve learned about keeping them happy.

Whether you’re buying your very first succulent or planning a whole garden, we’re here to help you choose well and grow with confidence.

Lalazar Nursery is owned by Muhammad Talha Butt and offers plants, cut flowers and landscaping services from our spot on Lower Canal Road, Faisalabad — opposite Sitara Gold City Phase 2.
