Quick answers

Check the top 2–3 cm of soil with a finger: if it’s dry, water thoroughly until it drains. Succulents and cacti prefer to dry out fully between drinks; leafy tropicals like gently moist soil. Overwatering is the most common plant killer.

A spot near a window where the sun doesn’t hit the leaves directly — you should see a soft shadow, not a sharp one. East-facing windows are ideal for most houseplants.

Most often too much water or poor drainage. Make sure the pot has holes, let the soil dry a little more between waterings, and remove yellowed leaves so the plant can redirect energy.

When roots circle the pot or poke through the drainage holes — usually every 1–2 years in spring. Choose a pot only 2–5 cm wider and use fresh, well-draining mix.
